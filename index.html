    How these numbers work

    Profit and loss leaves out money you deposit, withdraw or move between Hyperliquid, HyperEVM and Ethereum. HyperCore uses Hyperliquid's own P&L history. Wallets and staked HYPE count only price moves on what you held at the time.

    Value uses Hyperliquid's account history for HyperCore, merged down to its finest detail. Transfers are placed at the exact minute from your transfer log, so money in transit isn't counted twice. Staked HYPE, HyperEVM and Ethereum balances are rebuilt from your transaction history and priced at each moment.

    Daily up / down is each day's profit or loss. Striped days are older than Hyperliquid's detailed history, so a few days share one estimate.

    Average entry for HyperCore spot is Hyperliquid's own cost basis. Tokens moved in from elsewhere count at the price when they arrived. Ethereum tokens use what you paid in the same transaction, or the market price when they arrived. HyperEVM and staked HYPE use the price when the balance went up. Sales lower the cost in proportion, so the average entry stays the same.

    Small balances under $20 are grouped at the bottom of Holdings and Where it sits.

    Data: Hyperliquid public API, HyperEVM and Ethereum public nodes, Blockscout for Ethereum history, CoinGecko for RAIL. Any ERC-20 token on Ethereum is found automatically. Listed coins are priced by CoinGecko, and DEX-only coins by GeckoTerminal when they have at least $20k of liquidity. Airdropped spam without real liquidity is hidden. On HyperEVM, every token that trades on HyperCore is checked, plus WHYPE and kHYPE.
